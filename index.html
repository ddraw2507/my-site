Who I Am

Born in the late ’90s, shaped by life in central Vietnam, and now based in the capital. Trained as an infrastructure engineer - in other words, I spend my days modeling bridges, roads, and tunnels. Beyond work, I stick to quiet rituals: unhurried walks, lingering dinners, and conversations that feel complete when they end.

iii. 🏗️

The Work

I lead a small BIM team in Hanoi, serving clients across Europe - Italy mostly, but also Germany, France, Spain, the UK. Six years into the construction industry, I’ve come to see two very different versions of myself: the first three years and everything that followed. What used to take me eight hours now takes four. Not because I do less but because I’ve learned to work faster, smarter, and more reliably. It sounds simple. It wasn’t.
